About me

I am an anthropologist and filmmaker exploring the limits of what is possible.

I hold a Bachelors and Masters degree in Cultural Anthropology and a Masters degree in Communication Sciences and Digital Media. With an interest in sustainability, urban change and hope, my research and art has been mostly focused in exploring the different kinds of futures we imagine and propose in the 21st century.

I invite you to look into my award winning research projects and world touring exhibited audiovisual projects.
