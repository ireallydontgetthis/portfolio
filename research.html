Research

  • Futures of the Mapocho River

    2026

    Project for Masters thesis, where I observed how urban futures emerge in the city of Santiago around its main river, the Mapocho River. By observing perspectives from different age groups, professions and sectors of the city, I explored how dreams of a better future for the city are met in the real everyday experience In Santiago. Lastly, we explored the future of the city through collage workshops.

  • Solarpunk and the Value of Utopian Efforts

    2024

    Bachelor's thesis project at Leiden University, where I looked into the Solarpunk movement, its digital presence and why they imagine brigther and ecollogically just futures. By carrying out participant observation, I was able to understand the movement from within, and draft the first survey in the community with over 500 respondents. This research project will be published by the Cambridge Journal of Human Behavour in Autumn 2026.
